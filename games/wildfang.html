A FIELD GUIDE

Trust your instincts.

Your tail is a wall. Close the loop.

Steer with WASD / arrows or swipe and drag. Keep moving around a foe, then touch or cross your own tail. A closed loop captures every enemy your armor can hold. Crossing yourself is safe.

Read the jaws before you commit.

Green jaw numbers are at or below your armor: your body blocks and wears them down. Orange jaws can chew through your tail. Their orange ring fills before a cut. » marks a foe faster than you. Use a well-timed turn or surge.

Surge to surround. Harden to hold.

SPACE / Surge gives a short speed burst, not a damaging attack. E / Harden temporarily adds 3 armor. Harden just before closing on a tough foe, or while its “too strong” warning is showing. Your face is always vulnerable.

Length is your life.

Food regrows your body. Head hits take at least 4 segments, usually a percentage of your length. Cuts sever the rear of your tail; collect the scraps quickly. Below 8 segments, the run ends. Each level sheds 18% (down to 24) for more base armor and speed.

One expedition. Fifteen minutes.

Forage in refuges; risk hunting grounds for higher scores. Aim for level 10 by dawn. Four optional wardens test larger loops and stronger scales. Compare local scores and replay the same world.
